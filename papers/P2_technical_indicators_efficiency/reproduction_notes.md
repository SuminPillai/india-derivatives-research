# P2 Reproduction Notes

## Environment

- Python 3.13, dependencies in the top-level `requirements.txt` (uses `backtrader`).
- Equity OHLC for the 78-stock universe staged in MongoDB (`MONGO_URI`, default
  `mongodb://localhost:27017/`).
- `np.random.seed(20260509)` fixed for the stationary-bootstrap resampling.

## Pipeline

`01_run_strategies.py` backtests all 13 configurations, aggregates per-stock returns to the
strategy level, computes annualised Sharpe ratios, and runs the Romano–Wolf StepM stepdown
correction (stationary bootstrap, family-wise error rate 5%). Survivors are the strategies
with adjusted p < 0.05.

## Result summary (regenerated by the pipeline)

- 8 of 13 configurations survive the Romano–Wolf correction.
- Survivors: 3 SMA crossovers, 2 MACD variants, 2 Donchian breakouts, and RSI_20_80
  (adjusted p = 0.037).
- Best configuration: Donchian_20, annualised Sharpe 1.26, adjusted p = 0.0006.
- Non-survivors: remaining RSI variants, both Bollinger Band configurations, and the
  multi-indicator confluence strategy (adjusted p between 0.071 and 0.473).

## Sample

78 NSE F&O-eligible stocks, 1 January 2015 – 30 April 2025.
